How to play

  • Knock down one bottle and you score its number.
  • Knock down several and you score how many fell.
  • A bottle only counts if it's lying on the grass, not resting on another bottle or the stick.
  • Fallen bottles are stood up where they landed, so the bottles spread out.
  • Reach the target exactly to win (50 in the official game, or 30 or 20 for a shorter one). Go over and you drop to half the target: 25, 15 or 10.
  • Three misses in a row and you're out.

Throwing: the stick is thrown underhand and upright. Press and drag down to swing back, then flick up and let go. A faster flick throws further. Where you press left or right of centre sets your aim.
